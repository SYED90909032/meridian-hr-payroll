# ADR-006: Single-admin approval exception

Blueprint ref: D6
Status: Accepted (provisional)

## Decision
Owner may approve with step-up, 24h release delay and a second-contact notice; own line flagged.

## Reason
Keeps one-person companies usable with recorded risk.

## Rejected alternative
Bar small companies
