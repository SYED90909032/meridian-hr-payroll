# ADR-003: Pack assurance gate L0 to L3

Blueprint ref: D3
Status: Accepted (provisional)

## Decision
Live payroll requires an L2 or L3 pack. The UAE pack ships at L0. Sandbox and parallel-run only. Enforced by a DB trigger and an engine check.

## Reason
Honest assurance claim; no unreviewed statutory logic moves money.

## Rejected alternative
Self-attested packs
